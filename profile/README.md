# Data Science for Health Equity (DSxHE)

**Building a world where data improves everyone's health.**

DSxHE is a cross-sector, cross-discipline community of academics, health and care professionals, policy-makers and enthusiasts using data science to advance health equity. Members come from academia, the public sector, private industry, funding bodies, public media and the third sector. The community is volunteer-led, with part-time support from a funded Community Manager.

🌐 [datascienceforhealthequity.com](https://www.datascienceforhealthequity.com/)

## Our goals

- **Create community:** connect the health equity and data science worlds through events, opportunity sharing, and a space to learn and network.
- **Raise awareness:** show how data science can advance health equity, how it can also cause harm, and how to mitigate that harm.
- **Improve individual practice:** support real-world impact through research translation, methodological development and effective tools.
- **Influence institutional action:** advocate for policy change and better standards and practice among institutions and policy-makers.
